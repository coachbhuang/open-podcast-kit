# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy covers the Open Podcast Kit Interview Prep plugin, published by Yizhi Huang.

## Summary

The publisher does not run a server for this plugin and does not receive, collect, sell or share the information you use with it. The plugin contains only instructions and reference text. It has no analytics and runs no programs of its own.

## Information the plugin handles

When you use the plugin, Claude may read or create:

- your show details, such as show name, host name, audience and guest benefits;
- guest details you provide or Claude finds in public sources, such as name, employer, education, career history, public interviews and contact notes;
- drafts, such as research notes, interview questions, question sheets, outreach messages and a follow-up log.

## Where information is stored

When Claude has file access, drafts are saved in your working folder, including `show-profile.md` and `guests/<name>/`. Without file access, they stay in your Claude conversation. You control those files and can edit or delete them at any time.

## Where information may go

- **Claude:** information in your conversations and files you share is processed by Claude under your Claude account settings and Anthropic's terms and privacy policy.
- **Web search:** when Claude has web tools, guest research may send search terms containing a guest's name, employer or other public details to the search service in use.
- **Optional transcript tool:** a third-party tool may be suggested when no transcript is available. It is not included, and Claude must ask before you install or use it. That tool and the sites it contacts have their own terms and privacy practices.
- **People you message:** the plugin only drafts messages. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself.

## Retention and deletion

The publisher retains no data from this plugin. Files in your working folder remain until you delete them. Conversation retention follows your Claude account settings.

## Your responsibilities

Guests may have privacy rights. Collect only what you need, respect their requests, and do not publish private details without permission.

## Children

This plugin is not intended for people under 18.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published in this repository. For questions, open an issue at <https://github.com/coachbhuang/open-podcast-kit/issues>.
